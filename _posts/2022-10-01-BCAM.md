I'm happy to announce that I'm starting a new position as an [Ikerbasque Research Fellow](https://calls.ikerbasque.net/images/stories/2022_ikerbasque_research_fellowships_cofund_wolfram2.pdf) at the [Basque Center for Applied Mathematics](http://www.bcamath.org/en/), where I will join the ['Mathematical, Computational and Experimental Neuroscience'](http://www.bcamath.org/en/research/lines/MCEN) team.

I'm really excited to start this new period in a position that will allow to start new long-term research lines and projects! My [research line](https://www.ikerbasque.net/eu/miguel-aguilera) will include nonequilibrium aspects of neural computation, information thermodynamics of autonomous agents and research at the interface of statistical physics, complex systems and enactive cognition.
